Weather Patterns

Seasonal Cycles

Weather follows seasonal patterns driven by Earth's tilt, affecting temperature and precipitation.

Weather Forecasting

Data Collection

Satellites, radar, and ground stations collect temperature, humidity, and wind data.

Modeling

Supercomputers run numerical models to predict future weather based on current data.

Accuracy

Forecasts are most reliable within 7 days, with accuracy decreasing for longer ranges.

AI improves predictions by analyzing vast datasets for patterns.
